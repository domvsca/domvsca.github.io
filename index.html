Voltaage

I co-founded Voltaage in 2022 and designed all our products. Our platform holds data on thousands of charging points and serves hundreds of cities in France and Italy.

Fantaculo

In Italian fantasy football, played on real Serie A results, everyone blames luck. Fantaculo measures how much each result in your league owed to luck.

We started it in 2020. I wrote the first version alone and was its CTO until 2022. Around that measure we built tools for auctions, line-ups and trades, and in 2024 an AI assistant, FantaGPT.

The name comes from Italian slang, where culo means luck.

Also

From 2018 to 2021 I worked at Reply, an Italian consulting company, where I built machine-learning models and software for connected devices. That included the IoT middleware for a vehicle-to-grid project, in which parked electric cars send power back to the grid.

IGLOO is a smart lamp that shows how much electricity each appliance in a home uses. The prototype, built in 2018 and 2019, was entered in the James Dyson Award and in Illumia's Green Energy contest.

In 2022 I made the live visuals for a Napoleone concert at Hiroshima Mon Amour, a music club in Turin: seven scenes of 3D shapes, written in p5.js. I controlled them from a keyboard, and a webcam and a microphone made them react to the people and the music in the room.
